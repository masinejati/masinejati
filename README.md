## 🔬 Research: CARDIO-ML-ED

**Multimodal clinical ECG dataset for machine learning**

I contributed to the CARDIO-ML-ED project under the supervision of **Dr. Seyed Mohammad Hossein Moattar**. My work included digitizing printed ECG records, systematically organizing patient files, matching ECG recordings with their associated clinical records, and identifying unusable or irregular recordings for record-list updates.

The published **Version 2 dataset describes clinical records from 574 patients** across two medical centers. This is the published dataset size; my work involved data preparation rather than model training or clinical interpretation.

- **Published research dataset:** [CARDIO-ML-ED on Mendeley Data](https://data.mendeley.com/datasets/zfszfmpb82/2)
- **Dataset DOI:** [10.17632/zfszfmpb82.2](https://doi.org/10.17632/zfszfmpb82.2)
- **Associated manuscript:** *CARDIO-ML-ED Dataset: A Multi-modal Clinical ECG Database for Machine Learning Applications and Data Analysis* — **under review at Discover Computing** (not yet published).
- **Role:** Dataset contributor and research-manuscript co-author.
